Manufacturing DMAIC Defect Reduction

Extend a quality investigation through a validated improvement pilot and sustained control plan.

Planned study
DMAICRoot-cause analysisSPC

The engineering problem

A repeat manufacturing defect requires a measurable quality characteristic, a reliable baseline, and evidence that a corrective action addresses its cause.

The approach

  1. Define and measure

    Set the CTQ, scope, sampling plan, measurement-system checks, and baseline defect rate.

  2. Analyze and improve

    Prioritize defect modes with Pareto analysis, investigate causes, and test a focused corrective action.

  3. Sustain control

    Document an owner, monitoring frequency, reaction plan, and independent validation period.

Planned deliverables

This study is planned. A future report will distinguish baseline performance, pilot effects, and sustained results.

Data & interpretation

The existing SPC project provides analytical groundwork but does not establish a completed defect-reduction pilot.

The next engineering step

Choose a specific defect and establish a measurement plan.
