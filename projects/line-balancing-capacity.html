Production Line Balancing & Capacity Optimization

Study station workloads, compare cycle time with takt, and evaluate work allocation and capacity.

Planned study
Time studyTakt timeLine balancing

The engineering problem

Uneven workloads can constrain a line even when total labor capacity appears adequate. This study will identify the limiting station and assess alternative allocations.

The approach

  1. Measure the current state

    Collect station times, demand, available production time, task precedence, and staffing constraints.

  2. Identify the constraint

    Compare cycle times with takt and calculate baseline capacity and balance delay.

  3. Evaluate a future state

    Assess task reallocation and staffing alternatives while preserving task precedence and required quality checks.

Planned deliverables

A future analysis will include a line-balance chart, proposed allocation, and validated capacity comparison.

Data & interpretation

No throughput, labor savings, or return-on-investment improvement is claimed before measurement.

The next engineering step

Collect a representative station-level time study.
