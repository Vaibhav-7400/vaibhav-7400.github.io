Manufacturing / Decision support

Manufacturing KPI & Quality Intelligence Dashboard

Connect production, defect, and downtime data into a practical view of manufacturing performance.

Planned study
SQLPower BIData modeling

The engineering problem

Separate production and quality reports make it difficult to see whether a performance issue comes from availability, operating speed, or defects.

The approach

  1. Define the data model

    Connect production, quality, and equipment facts with consistent lot, machine, product, shift, and date keys.

  2. Make measures consistent

    Specify denominators and time windows for first-pass yield, scrap, throughput, downtime, and OEE.

  3. Support decisions

    Create production and quality drilldowns, trends, and a documented approach to investigating exceptions.

Planned deliverables

This planned project will produce a manufacturing data model, documented KPI definitions, and interactive reporting views.

Data & interpretation

The dashboard has not yet been implemented. Example KPI values will be labeled as sample data.

The next engineering step

Create production, quality, and equipment tables and validate the KPI definitions.
