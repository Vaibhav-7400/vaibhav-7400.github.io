Pathways to Progress: Global Socio-Economic Analysis

An interactive Tableau story exploring economic growth, connectivity, health, education, and inequality across countries.

Published Tableau story
TableauData visualizationDashboard storytelling

The engineering problem

Economic, health, education, and living-standard indicators tell different parts of a country's development story. This project brings those dimensions into an interactive visual narrative so readers can compare patterns across countries and over time.

The approach

  1. Compare growth and connectivity

    Combine GDP-per-capita trends and a world map with electricity access and Internet usage to examine economic growth alongside infrastructure and connectivity.

  2. Explore development dimensions

    Organize health, emissions, and development into a dedicated story chapter, then examine literacy and schooling in an education-focused chapter.

  3. Examine living standards

    Use a fourth chapter to bring living standards and inequality into the global comparison.

  4. Guide an interactive investigation

    Use a four-part Tableau story, country and year filters, and top/bottom ranking controls to let readers explore the relationships rather than rely on a single static chart.

What the project delivers

The published visualization combines maps, time-series views, rankings, and interactive filters in a four-chapter story. It demonstrates how Tableau dashboards can turn multiple indicators into a coherent narrative for a broad audience.

Data & interpretation

Country selections, time periods, and aggregation affect the comparisons. The visualizations describe patterns; they do not establish causal relationships between indicators. The live workbook is the source for displayed values.

The next engineering step

Extend the story with selected-country comparisons, concise annotations, and additional documentation of indicator sources.
