Semiconductor / Factory operations

Fab Scheduling & WIP Optimization

Compare dispatching rules, queue behavior, and bottleneck utilization in a simulated wafer-fab flow.

Planned study
SimulationSchedulingPython

The engineering problem

Re-entrant routes, variable tool availability, and priority lots make fab scheduling a tradeoff between throughput, cycle time, and due-date performance.

The approach

  1. Define the system

    Describe routes, lot sizes, tool capacity, setups, and assumptions about downtime.

  2. Compare policies

    Evaluate FIFO, shortest processing time, earliest due date, and bottleneck-aware rules under equivalent simulation conditions.

  3. Measure the tradeoffs

    Compare WIP, queue time, cycle time, utilization, and on-time completion across repeated simulation runs.

Planned deliverables

The related repository currently describes a production-scheduling concept. No implemented fab simulation or validated lead-time reduction is presented.

Data & interpretation

Expected improvements in the outline are targets rather than measured outcomes.

The next engineering step

Build a reproducible baseline model and add a scheduling comparison.
