Semiconductor Wafer Yield & Process Optimization

Investigate yield loss by tool and chamber, assess stability, and evaluate a more reliable operating window.

Planned study
SPCDOEPython

The engineering problem

A proposed wafer-process study will examine whether tool, chamber, and recipe differences explain a decline in yield.

The approach

  1. Establish the baseline

    Define wafer-level yield and defect measures, capture lot and tool identifiers, and distinguish real data from synthetic examples.

  2. Investigate variation

    Review trends, defect Pareto, tool/chamber comparisons, control charts, and capability under appropriate distribution assumptions.

  3. Test the process window

    Use a designed experiment to evaluate parameter interactions and validate a proposed recipe against an independent baseline.

Planned deliverables

This page defines a future case study. Analysis artifacts, measured results, and a repository will be linked when available.

Data & interpretation

No yield gains, capability improvements, or completed DOE results are claimed.

The next engineering step

Create a reproducible wafer-level dataset and document the initial yield baseline.
